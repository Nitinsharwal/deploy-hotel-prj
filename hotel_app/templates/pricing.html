Frequently asked questions

How does billing work?

You're billed monthly in advance for paid plans. The first charge appears on your vendor dashboard with a 7-day grace period to pay before listings are paused.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from the vendor dashboard. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle so you keep paid features until then.

What's the platform commission?

We take a small percentage of each confirmed booking (10% on Free, 7% on Pro, 3% on Enterprise). It's deducted automatically from your payout.

What happens if I exceed my plan limit?

Existing hotels, rooms, and images stay live. You won't be able to add new ones until you upgrade or delete existing items.
